In this part of the study, you will submit a donation decision for each of the 60 charities you just rated. The donation decision consists of (1) the number of slider tasks you commit to perform for the charity and (2) whether you want the donation to be Anonymous or Public.


To gain money for a charity, you will have to perform one or more slider tasks. The more tasks you perform correctly, the more money you will gain for the charity. The task(s) will be carried out in the second phase of the study (i.e., you will not be compensated with course credit for the time you spend carrying them out).


For each charity you will need to decide the number of slider tasks you would like to perform. For each charity, you can commit to performing any number of slider tasks between 0 and {{ max_tasks }}.


Only one of your 60 donation decisions will be randomly selected for implementation at the end of the experiment. That is, you will only complete the tasks you commit to for one of the 60 charities. Therefore, you should treat each one of the 60 donation decisions as being the only one that counts, instead of spreading the tasks across donations to several charities. All 60 donation decisions have the same probability of being chosen.


All charities selected for this study are highly rated for financial health, accountability, and transparency by Charity Navigator. Charity Navigator is a non-profit organization whose ratings seek to show donors how efficiently a charity will use their support.


The research team will send the selected charity the money you gain for it.

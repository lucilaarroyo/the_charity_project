Each potential donation can be either Anonymous or Public. You will decide which type of donation to make for each charity.

If chosen for implementation:

  • An Anonymous donation will have a 50% chance of being doubled by the researchers. An Anonymous donation will not be associated with your name.

  • A Public donation will have no possibility of being doubled. However, if the number of slider tasks you perform correctly for a Public donation is large enough, your name will be included on a "Donation Hero's" list on The Charity Project's website. The link to the website is on the Study Information page on SONA and will also be emailed to all participants that complete this study.

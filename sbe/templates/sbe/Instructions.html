For this task you have been paired with another participant to complete an advice-decision task for {{ num_actual_rounds }} rounds. One of you has been randomly assigned the role of Advisor and the other the role of Decider. On each round, both of you will be shown various dots dispersed across the screen and must estimate if the number of dots is LESS or MORE than 20. The dots will appear on the screen for {{ dots_secs }} {% is dots_secs == 1 %} second. {% else %} seconds. {% endif %}


In half of the rounds the Decider will make this decision on their own and in the other half the Advisor will provide a recommendation that the Decider has to implement. For each incorrect advice submitted by the Advisor, the Decider will have to complete {{ round_multiplier }} additional rounds of the same task on their own once the original {{ num_actual_rounds }} have finished. The Advisor will not have to complete any additional rounds in the task.


You have been assigned the role of {{ role }}.

{% if role == "Decider" %} In rounds without advice, you will be asked to submit a decision about the dots, and receive feedback about accuracy. In rounds with advice, you will be shown the Advisor's recommendation, be forced to provide the answer given by the Advisor, receive feedback about whether the advice was correct or not, and whether you will need to complete additional rounds of the task as a result. {% else %} In rounds without advice, you will not be asked to submit a response. In rounds with advice, you will have to submit a recommendation that will determine the Decider's decision for the round. The advice will be shown to the Decider, who will be forced to submit the recommended response. After every advice round, you will be told if the advice was correct or not, and whether the Decider will need to complete additional rounds of the task as a result. {% endif %}

You will not know if it is a round without or with advice until AFTER the dots are shown on the screen, so you will need to pay attention to the screen in every round.

You will now complete {{ num_trial_rounds }} practice rounds to get a better sense of the task before the official rounds begin. You will not be told whether your responses are correct or incorrect in the practice rounds.

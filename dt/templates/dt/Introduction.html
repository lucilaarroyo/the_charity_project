In this task you will have the opportunity to gain real money for a charity.


This task has two phases. You need to complete the first phase to earn course credit. The second phase is optional and can last up to 20 minutes. The second phase will not be compensated with course credit, instead, you would be donating your time and effort to gain money for a charity of your liking.


In the first phase, you will submit a donation commitment for each of {{num_charities}} charities. To gain money for a charity, you will have to complete one or more slider tasks. The more tasks you complete correctly, the more money you will gain for the charity. A donation commitment specifies how many slider tasks you would like to complete for a particular charity. For each charity, you will be shown its name, a picture, and its mission, and you will be asked how many slider tasks you want to commit to that charity. For each charity, you can commit to any number of slider tasks between 0 (no tasks) and {{ max_tasks }}.


In the second phase, one of your {{num_charities}} donation commitments will be randomly selected. For the charity chosen, you will be asked to perform your donation commitment. That is, you will only complete the tasks you commit to one of the {{num_charities}} charities. Therefore, you should treat every donation commitments as if it is the only one that counts (i.e., the one that will be selected), instead of spreading the tasks commitments across charities. All {{num_charities}} donation commitments have the same probability of being chosen. You will not be compensated with course credit for the time you spend performing the slider tasks you commit to.


All charities selected for this task are highly rated by Charity Navigator — a non-profit organization whose ratings seek to show donors how efficiently a charity will use their support.


The research team will send the money you gain, by correctly completing the slider tasks, to your selected charity.

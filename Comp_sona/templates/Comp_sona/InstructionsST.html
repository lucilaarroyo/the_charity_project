You will have the opportunity to gain money for the charities you just rated.
All charities selected for this study are highly rated for financial health, accountability, and transparency by Charity Navigator. Charity Navigator is a non-profit organization whose ratings seek to show donors how efficiently a charity will use their support.

You will be presented with the 60 charities once again. For each charity you will need to decide whether or not you will want to complete a short task at the end of the study in exchange for money being sent to that charity.

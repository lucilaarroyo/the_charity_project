In this part of the study, you will have the opportunity to gain money for a charity by correctly performing one or more slider tasks. The more tasks you complete correctly, the more money you will gain for the charity. The task(s) will be carried out in the second part of the study (i.e., you will not be compensated with course credit for the time you spend carrying the tasks out).

You will be presented again with the 60 charities you just rated. For each charity you will need to decide the number of slider tasks you would like to perform in exchange for money being sent to that charity. For each charity, you can commit to performing any number of slider tasks between 0 and {{ max_tasks }}. One of your 60 donation decisions will be randomly selected for implementation at the end of the experiment. That is, you will only complete the tasks you commit to for one of the 60 charities. Therefore, you should treat each one of the 60 donation decisions as being the only one that counts, instead of spreading the tasks across donations to several charities. All 60 donation decisions have the same probability of being chosen.

All charities selected for this study are highly rated for financial health, accountability, and transparency by Charity Navigator. Charity Navigator is a non-profit organization whose ratings seek to show donors how efficiently a charity will use their support.

The research team will send the selected charity the sum of money you gain for it.

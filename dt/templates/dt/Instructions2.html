The money you gain for a charity will depend on the number of slider tasks you perform correctly in the second phase.

Each task consists of 5 sliders randomly distributed across the screen. All sliders begin positioned at 0. You will have 30 seconds to position all sliders at 50. If all sliders are positioned at 50 when the 30-second timer runs out, you will gain a corresponding amount of money for the selected charity.

Each potential donation can be classified as either Anonymous or Public. You will decide how to classify any donation you choose to make.

  • Your total of Anonymous donations will have a 50% chance of being doubled by the researchers. Anonymous donations will not be associated with your name.

  • Public donations will have no possibility of being doubled. However, if your total of Public donations is large enough, your name will be included in The Charity Project's website. The link to the website is on the Study Information page on SONA and will also be emailed to all participants that complete this study.

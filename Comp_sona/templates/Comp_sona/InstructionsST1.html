Each potential donation will be classified as either Anonymous or Public. You will know the classification before you have to submit your choice. Half will be Anonymous and half will be Public.

  • The total amount of your Anonymous donations will have a 50% chance of being doubled by the researchers. Anonymous donations will not be associated with your name or any identifiable information.

  • Public donations will have no possibility of being doubled. However, if the total amount of your Public donations is large enough, your name will be included on The Charity Project's website. The link to the website is on the Study Information page on SONA and will also be emailed to all participants that complete this study.

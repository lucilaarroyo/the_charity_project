One of the 60 donation decisions will be selected at random and implemented at the end of the experiment. This means that it is not possible to spread the endowment across different charities. Therefore, you should treat each decision as an unique opportunity to donate whatever portion of the endowment you would like to donate.

The selected charity will be sent the corresponding donation, and whatever portion of the endowment is not donated will be yours to keep (i.e., will be added to your total earnings for participating in the experiment).


Each donation decision will be classified as Anonymous or Public.

  • Anonymous donation decisions will have 50% chance of being doubled by the researchers if chosen for implementation. However, the donation will not be associated with your name or any identifiable information.

  • Public donation decisions will have no possibility of being doubled. However, with your consent and if your donation is large enough, you will be listed as a charity heroe on the following website linked to the Lead Researcher's Laboratory: The Charity Project.
